
# Definitions
- **Semantic** -- The broader branch of linguistics concerned with the exact meaning of words, how their meanings change, and how they relate to other words

## Interesting alternatives
* **Captivating** — holds your attention completely.
* **Fascinating** — intensely interesting or charming.
* **Intriguing** — arouses curiosity or suspense.
* **Engaging** — keeps you involved and focused.
* **Compelling** — forces your attention or belief.
* **Gripping** — takes a powerful hold on you.
* **Absorbing** — takes up all your attention.
* **Provocative** — stimulates deep thought or anger.
* **Enthralling** — captures your attention like a spell.
* **Riveting** — locks your attention to one spot.
* **Thought-provoking** — makes you think deeply.
* **Stimulating** — sparks active interest or excitement.
* **Remarkable** — worthy of being noticed or mentioned.
* **Exceptional** — unusually good or unique.
* **Curious** — unusual enough to invite investigation.
* **Striking** — attracts attention by being unusual.
* **Mind-boggling** — overwhelming to the intellect.
* **Appealing** — attractive, inviting, or pleasing.
* **Illuminating** — clarifies or sheds new light.
* **Beguiling** — charming or diverting in an attractive way.
* **Spellbinding** — holds your attention as if by magic.
* **Mesmerizing** — hypnotizing or freezing your attention completely.
* **Arresting** — striking enough to catch the eye instantly.
* **Exotic** — strikingly unusual or excitingly different.
* **Piquing** — sharply stimulating your curiosity or pride.
* **Ingenious** — cleverly inventive, resourceful, or original.
* **Novel** — strikingly new, unusual, or original.
* **Astonishing** — causing great surprise, wonder, or amazement.
* **Unprecedented** — completely new and never done before.
* **Spectacular** — dramatic, daring, or thrilling to watch.
