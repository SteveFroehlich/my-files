## Minimalism 

Phase 1: The Mindset Shift (Read First)

Before you pick up a single trash bag, you need to change how you view your belongings so you do not feel guilty letting them go.

- **Goodbye, Things by Fumio Sasaki**
    - _Why you need it:_ It completely redefines the relationship between your self-worth and your objects.
    - _The big takeaway:_ It helps you realize that your possessions do not define your identity or happiness.
- **The Life-Changing Magic of Tidying Up by Marie Kondō**
    - _Why you need it:_ It shifts your focus from what to _throw away_ to what you want to _keep_.
    - _The big takeaway:_ Keeping only items that actively "spark joy" removes the guilt of discarding expensive or gifted items.

🔨 Phase 2: The Practical Guides (Read Second)

Once your mindset is ready, these books give you the literal blueprint, checklists, and physical steps to clear the rooms.

- **The Minimalist Home by Joshua Becker**
    - _Why you need it:_ It is a highly structured, room-by-room manual written for everyday people and families.
    - _The big takeaway:_ It provides clear instructions on how to handle specific spaces (like kitchens or garages) and explains _why_ we let clutter pile up in those specific areas.
- **Minimalista by Shira Gill**
    - _Why you need it:_ It offers a highly organized, five-step framework (Clarify, Edit, Organize, Elevate, Maintain) that applies to any toolkit.
    - _The big takeaway:_ It breaks overwhelming projects down into bite-sized, 15-minute wins so you never experience decluttering burnout.
