
Wake up science 
To align your wake-up with peer-reviewed sleep science, start with the **Rise Science App**. [[1](https://www.risescience.com/)]

- **The Clinical Backing:** Rather than focusing on a single morning alarm, Rise was built by Northwestern University sleep engineers around the two primary principles heavily validated by sleep science: **sleep debt** and **circadian rhythms**. [[1](https://yespress.io/rise-science)]
- **How It Helps You Wake Naturally:** It pulls your historical Apple Health data to pinpoint your exact "Melatonin Window" and predicts your natural groginess window (sleep inertia). It tells you precisely _when_ your body is biologically ready to wake up, minimizing the cortisol spikes caused by catching you in the wrong phase. [[1](https://www.risescience.com/), [2](https://www.instagram.com/p/Da3-fdwlAXe/), [3](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wonderwake-reloj-despertador/id6475820849), [4](https://www.risescience.com/blog/chronotype), [5](https://www.instagram.com/p/DW4zxa7mUTG/)]
