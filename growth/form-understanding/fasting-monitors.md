## 💡 Tips for Beginner Tracking Success

To get accurate data while fasting, follow these simple setup habits:

- The 5-Minute Rule: For blood pressure, always sit quietly in a chair with your feet flat on the floor for 5 minutes _before_ pressing start. Talking or moving will artificially spike your reading.
- Wash Your Hands First: If using a finger-prick glucometer, always wash your hands with warm water and soap and dry them completely. Leftover residue from lotions or touching food can heavily distort your glucose reading.
