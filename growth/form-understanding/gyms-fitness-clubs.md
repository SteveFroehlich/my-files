
The Chicago and Arlington Heights areas feature several premier fitness communities and social clubs where you can find this exact same demographic.

The social dynamics change depending on the location: Chicago options lean heavily into premium, career-focused luxury wellness, while the Arlington Heights community is driven by highly dedicated boutique neighborhood studios.

## Luxury Fitness Hubs & Reformer Studios

- [Equinox](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/12m9jk4p3): The ultimate lifestyle club in Chicago. People work on laptops in the lounge, grab post-workout juices, and socialize.
    
    - [Equinox Gold Coast](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/12m9jk4p3) (900 N Michigan Ave)
    - [Equinox Lincoln Common](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11h3jhp5xq) (2355 N Lincoln Ave) [1, 2, 3] 
    
- [solidcore]: High-intensity, low-impact strength training that draws the exact same demographic looking for a intense, boutique fitness environment.
    
    - [solidcore Boystown](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11h1yffzdk) (3312 N Broadway)
    - [solidcore River North](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11f_kp4wq3) (463 W Chicago Ave)
    - [solidcore West Loop](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11h4f_x1lv) (7 S Halsted St) [4, 5, 6, 7] 
    

## High-Energy HIIT & Social Run Clubs

- Barry's: Famous for its "Red Room" HIIT classes, high energy, and community-driven customer base.
    
    - [Barry's River North](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11csqlnst7) (11 W Erie St)
    - [Barry's Lincoln Park](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11dx91_0v9) (1937 N Halsted St) [8, 9] 
    
- Community Run Clubs: If you want a casual setting where chatting is expected, modern community run clubs are highly popular options.
    
    - [Lakeview Run Club](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/11qt422yfh): Meets weekly at Nettelhorst School (3252 N Broadway) for all paces and socializes afterward.
    - [Fleet Feet Old Town](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=/g/1hc4zx100) (1706 N Wells St): Hosts group runs on Monday and Thursday nights, serving as a huge anchor for the local running community. [10, 11, 12]
