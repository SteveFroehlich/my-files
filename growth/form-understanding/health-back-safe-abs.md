
Safer Core Alternatives

If you want to build a strong core without damaging your spinal discs, switch to exercises that keep your spine in a neutral, supported position: [[1](https://blog.renewwellness.care/exercises-to-avoid-with-low-back-pain)]

- **Planks**: Builds deep core endurance while keeping your spine straight and stable.
- **Dead Bugs**: Trains abdominal control while your lower back remains safely supported by the floor.
- **Bird-Dogs**: Strengthens the core and lower back muscles through limb movement without bending the spine.
- **McGill Curl-Ups**: Developed by spine expert Dr. Stuart McGill, this alternative targets the abs while preserving the natural curve of your lower back. [[1](https://www.health.harvard.edu/staying-healthy/want-a-stronger-core-skip-the-sit-ups), [2](https://www.njspineandortho.com/safe-exercises-for-herniated-disk-back-pain/), [3](https://spineandpainla.com/are-sit-ups-bad-for-your-back/), [4](https://www.cornercanyonhealth.com/why-sit-ups-are-bad-for-your-back-and-the-right-exercise-to-try-instead/), [5](https://blog.renewwellness.care/exercises-to-avoid-with-low-back-pain)]
