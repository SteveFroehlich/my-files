## Concepts

Allow people to get to no. It protects their need for autonomy 

Get to “that’s right” before any real problem solving can begin.

3 types of yes:
1. Commitment yes - the kind you want - this leads to action
2. Affirmation yes - somewhat meaningless 
3. Avoidance yes - counterpart says just to get out of the convo. They will back out later

Concepts and tools in the book allow negotiators to get past their emotions so the “Getting to yes” by Roger Fisher dynamic is possible.
