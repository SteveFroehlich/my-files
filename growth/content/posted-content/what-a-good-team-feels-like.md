# What a good software engineering team feels like

It’s like making music, where each person is rifting off the others work adding their own 
unique contribution to the melody that delights the audience (users) and performers 
(developers) alike. One person’s achievements motivate and trigger others. Its tough to 
always have this, but its magic when it happens.

Something I’m trying to keep in mind as we make our 2025 plans. If this resonates let me 
know what the best team you were on was like.


## additional thoughts
Like an improv jazz band. People just pick up an instrument and play.
