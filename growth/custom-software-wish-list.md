
## custom music app
Pulls audio from YouTube videos
Stores them locally or cloud.
Adds them to some performant player software
Features:
- create a playlist. 
- Play list in sequential or random order
- Allow for very long lists

## social and news aggregator
Pulls from Farcaster, LinkedIn, twitter, hacker-news, whatever else.

Sends an email beautifully formatted.

Let’s me make posts from markdown to each platform.
